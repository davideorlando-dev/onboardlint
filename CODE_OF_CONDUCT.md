# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and behaviour—not personal characteristics.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. To report a concern, use the private contact route listed in the repository's GitHub security or maintainer profile. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
